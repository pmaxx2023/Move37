Questions

Do my photos get uploaded?

No. Ink Trace does all the work inside your browser. Your photos stay on your device.

What do I get when I buy?

A download link by email. It is one file that opens in any modern browser, like Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox. It works without the internet.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It works on recent iPhones, iPads and Android phones. A computer is faster for large photos and batch mode.

Can I sell what I make?

With Pro, yes. You can sell prints, products and client work made with it. The Free and Full versions are for personal use.

What is batch mode?

Pick many photos at once. Pro applies the same settings to all of them and gives you one zip file of finished PNGs.

Can I use it with a Cricut or a laser cutter?

Pro saves the lines as an SVG file. Open it in Cricut Design Space, a laser cutter app, or a design app like Inkscape or Illustrator.

How big are the saved images?

Free saves at 1080 px on the long side. Full and Pro save at the size of your photo, up to 4096 px. A photo smaller than that saves at its own size.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Email us within 14 days of buying and we will refund you.
